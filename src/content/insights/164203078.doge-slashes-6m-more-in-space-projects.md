### **New Claimed Savings / Cuts from April**

This month’s report covers the savings and terminations recorded between 31 March and 28 April 2025. In total, **[Department of Government Enablement](https://www.linkedin.com/company/department-of-government-enablement/)** claims **$6,285,727** in savings across a small set of grants, contracts, and leases.

### **Claimed Savings: Cuts to Grants and Contracts**

The largest claimed cut was to **Bryce Space and Technology, LLC**, where $4,667,483 in funding was removed. Bryce was providing NASA leadership with independent diagnostics and strategic advisory work across technology, policy, and economics. Based on the contract’s structure, it appears to be a blanket agreement with a "potential value", meaning much of the savings reflect future services not yet delivered. **[Grant details here](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_80NSSC24FA979_8000_80NSSC24AA010_8000/)**.

The second-largest cut went to **[A3L Federal Works LLC.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/a3l-federal-works-llc-/)**, which lost $1,423,496. This contract had been providing knowledge management, event support, and communications services to NASA. **[Grant details here](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_80GSFC24CA024_8000_-NONE-_-NONE-/)**.

Other smaller cuts included:

*   **[FLORIDA FIRST ROBOTICS EDUCATION FOUNDATION INC](https://www.linkedin.com/company/florida-first-robotics-education-foundation-inc/).** – $60,300 cut for sponsorship of a regional robotics competition. **[Grant details here](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_80NSSC23PB058_8000_-NONE-_-NONE-/)**.
    
*   **[DIVERSIFIED SERVICES GROUP INCORPORATED](https://www.linkedin.com/company/diversified-services-group-incorporated/)** – $38,578 cut from a live plant maintenance services contract. **[Grant details here](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_80NSSC22PA482_8000_-NONE-_-NONE-/)**.
    
*   **Howard, Wershbale & Co** – $3,950 cut from financial statement review services. **[Grant details here](https://www.usaspending.gov/award/CONT_AWD_80NSSC20P1795_8000_-NONE-_-NONE-/)**.

### **Cuts without savings...**

An interesting trend is the large number of terminated contracts where **no savings** were reported. Highlights include:

*   A **NASA contract** with the **Universities Space Research Association**, valued at **$3,321,286**, was cancelled with a recorded savings of **$0**. The project, under the Science Activation Program, aimed to support planetary exploration and science engagement for the Science Mission Directorate (SMD). Despite its scale, its termination brought no financial savings.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with the **[University of Alaska Fairbanks](https://www.linkedin.com/school/university-of-alaska-fairbanks/)**, worth **$182,619**, was also terminated for convenience, again with a recorded savings of **$0**. This contract supported a two-day AG-style meeting of the Cross-AG EDIA Working Group, aiming to bring Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility (EDIA) principles to the planetary sciences community.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with the **[National Society of Black Physicists](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nsbpinc/)**, valued at **$99,751**, was cancelled with a savings of **$0**. The project involved organising a Student Leadership Development Summit (SLDS) and topical workshops to advance engagement in planetary science.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **[Virginia Tech](https://www.linkedin.com/school/virginia-tech/)** for **$763,904** was terminated, showing **no savings**. This interdisciplinary project aimed to democratise data for adaptation decision-making in the North-Atlantic Coastal Plain, focusing on hazards like land subsidence and compound flooding.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **[John Hopkins University](https://www.linkedin.com/company/john-hopkins-university/)**, valued at **$969,349**, was also terminated with no financial savings. This project focused on resolving intra-urban variability in heat and air quality from space and ground observations to support environmental justice initiatives.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **Chenega Global Protection, LLC**, valued at **$231,345**, was cancelled under a Termination for Convenience Agreement. The project had provided security police officer support for NASA's Earth Information Center (EIC) but was terminated without any savings recorded.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **[George Mason University](https://www.linkedin.com/school/george-mason-university/)**, valued at **$529,129**, was cancelled, again reporting no savings. The project addressed disparities in health and safety effects of dust storms across the United States under NASA's interdisciplinary research in earth sciences programme.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **[Brown University](https://www.linkedin.com/school/brown-university/)**, worth **$752,680**, was cancelled without generating any recorded savings. The project focused on using NASA Earth observations to support resilient community planning around California’s Salton Sea.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with the **[Natural Areas Conservancy](https://www.linkedin.com/company/natural-areas-conservancy/)**, valued at **$485,603**, was cancelled with no savings. The project assessed access to nature and related benefits in environmental justice communities across US cities.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **[Cornell University](https://www.linkedin.com/school/cornell-university/)**, valued at **$260,000**, was cancelled with a savings of **$0**. The contract supported an innovative workshop on Equity, Diversity, Inclusion, and Accessibility (EDIA) for leaders in planetary science.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **[NUBURU® Inc.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/nuburu/)**, worth **$849,989**, was cancelled without any financial savings. The project aimed to develop blue laser technology for planetary and lunar power management under an SBIR Phase II effort.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **[Unibap AB](https://www.linkedin.com/company/unibap/)**, worth **$70,000**, was terminated with no savings. The project involved development of the OSE-SAT in-space autonomy testbed.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with the **[UCAR - The University Corporation for Atmospheric Research](https://www.linkedin.com/company/university-corporation-for-atmospheric-research/)**, valued at **$14,000**, was terminated without savings. The project was linked to the DYNAMIC-X Phase A study focused on dynamical atmosphere-ionosphere coupling.
    
*   A **NASA contract** with **R3 UNIQ Inc**, valued at **$71,649**, was cancelled with no recorded savings. This project supported licenses and maintenance for the OMNeT network simulation software system.
    

None of these projects recorded any savings according to the DOGE database, raising major questions about how terminations are being counted and reported.
