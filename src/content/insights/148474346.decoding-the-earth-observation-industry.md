## Planet Labs’ Mission

Planet Labs is at the forefront of using space technology to drive positive change on Earth. With a mission to *"use space to help life on Earth,"* Planet Labs has developed the world's largest fleet of Earth-observation satellites, capturing daily, high-resolution images of our planet. These images are not just data points; they are powerful tools that enable actionable insights across a wide range of global challenges, from monitoring climate change to enhancing agricultural productivity and disaster management.

Planet Labs operates approximately 200 satellites, providing a unique and comprehensive view of the Earth's landmass every day. This vast collection of data is invaluable for a multitude of sectors, including agriculture, government, defence, and environmental conservation. The company's ability to deliver real-time, high-resolution imagery sets it apart in the Earth Observation industry, positioning it as a critical player in the push towards a more sustainable and secure world.

The data provided by Planet Labs is instrumental in enabling organizations to make informed, data-driven decisions:
* **Agriculture:** Farmers use this data to monitor crop health, optimize resource use, and improve yields.
* **Government & Civil Sectors:** This data aids in urban planning, infrastructure coordination, and environmental monitoring.

This analysis will not only spotlight Planet Labs but also set the stage for broader discussions on the evolving dynamics of the Earth Observation market and the role of satellite imagery in fostering global sustainability.

## History & Milestones

Planet Labs was founded in 2010 by former NASA scientists who shared a vision of using space technology to better understand and care for our planet. Their goal was to democratize access to satellite imagery by deploying a large fleet of low-cost, small satellites, known as Doves, to capture daily images of the Earth. This vision was rooted in the belief that timely, accurate information about global changes could empower businesses, governments, and civil society to make informed decisions and drive positive change.

Since launching their first Dove satellites in 2013, Planet Labs has achieved numerous milestones that have solidified their position as a leader in the Earth Observation industry:

## Technical Infrastructure & Analytics Architecture

Planet Labs stands out in the Earth Observation industry not just for the scale of its satellite fleet but also for its pioneering work in agile aerospace and data analytics. The company’s approach to satellite design and deployment—characterized by rapid iteration and innovation—has allowed it to maintain the largest Earth observation fleet in history, consisting of approximately 200 satellites. 

### Satellite Design

The company's technological advancements are reflected in its continuous development and launch of new satellite models, such as the **SuperDove** satellites, which provide higher resolution and more spectral bands than earlier models. Additionally, their **SkySat** constellation offers the highest commercially available intraday revisit capabilities, capturing imagery of a specific location up to ten times per day.

![Visual baseline contrasting the operational architectures of the SkySat and SuperDove satellite lines.](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/df3e1da2-2f9b-4e73-8c45-d8b86ea08421_681x226.png)

### AI and Data Analytics

Planet Labs also excels in data analytics, where it combines satellite imagery with AI and machine learning to generate actionable insights. This approach allows users to detect changes, monitor trends, and predict future outcomes more effectively than traditional methods.

Their use of AI in the *Planet Analytic Feeds* product, which automates land classification and change detection, has been particularly effective in sectors like urban planning, intelligence, and resource management. These analytics have helped clients make informed decisions faster, providing a significant competitive edge.

![The automated Planet Analytics interface demonstrating deep-learning computer vision for real-time vessel detection.](https://substack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/8527e080-be26-4f38-aa7a-e97fd5929cad_1200x843.png)
